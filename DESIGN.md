# Design
Operate mode. Phone used sitting down, often evening: charcoal #101713 background, ivory #f3f2e9 foreground, mint #b9ef79 action accent, muted #a8b4aa, line #2b382e. DM Sans for UI, Barlow Condensed for sports display. 4/8 spacing, maximum 1040px content, bottom nav on mobile, sidebar desktop. Flat editorial rows, numeric alignment, 44px minimum touch targets. No decorative imagery, gradients, glow or generic metric cards. Pitch is functional SVG. Primary action Continue, contextual action sheets, errors persist with recovery. Immediate navigation, subtle 120ms press feedback, reduced motion support.
